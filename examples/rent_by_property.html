Press play. The engine starts at FROM, not at SELECT — that gap between written order and real order is the whole point.

What it catches

INFOFilters are evaluated after the joins
In logical order, WHERE runs after the joins, so the joins conceptually carry unfiltered rows. Most optimizers push filters down automatically, but stacked views and some engines do not — worth knowing when a join feels slow.
WARNDISTINCT aggregate
COUNT(DISTINCT f.lease_id) forces its own distinct pass over the grouped rows before it can aggregate. On wide data this is often the single most expensive expression in the query.
INFOSort with no LIMIT
ORDER BY without LIMIT sorts the entire result set. If only the top rows matter, a LIMIT lets the engine use a much cheaper top-N sort.
Logical execution order, as taught by every SQL engine's documentation. QueryScope reads the query structure only — it does not connect to your database, and row counts depend on your data.
